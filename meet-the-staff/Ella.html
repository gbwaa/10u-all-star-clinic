10U All-Star Staff

Ella

Ella is a standout young athlete competing in 10U softball and currently playing for the South Hills Elite 9U travel team. She also serves as the ace pitcher for her Baldwin 10U softball team, where she consistently demonstrates leadership, composure, and determination in the circle. Ella’s strong arm, competitive drive, and confidence make her a key player and dependable teammate. Her dedication to the game and willingness to work hard continue to help her grow into one of the top young pitchers in her age group.

When she’s not on the softball field, Ella enjoys spending time with her dog and relaxing with family and friends. Her fun personality, love for the game, and positive attitude make her someone teammates enjoy being around both on and off the field. Ella’s passion for softball and natural athletic ability make her a player with tremendous potential and an exciting future ahead.
