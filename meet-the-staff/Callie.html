10U All-Star Staff

Callie

Callie is a talented young athlete currently playing travel softball for the Devil Dogs. As a catcher, she brings toughness, focus, and leadership to the field while helping guide her team from behind the plate. A dedicated and hardworking player, Callie has earned a spot on the Baldwin All-Star team an impressive five years in a row, showcasing her commitment to the game and continued growth as an athlete. Whether she's calling a game, supporting her teammates, or working to improve her skills, Callie brings energy, determination, and a love for softball wherever she goes. Her favorite softball player is Texas Tech standout Mia Williams, who inspires her passion for the sport and her goals as a player.

Off the field, Callie stays active by playing volleyball and basketball for Mother of Mercy Academy. Known by her nickname, "Peanut," she loves spending time at the ballpark watching her brother and his team play baseball. You can often find her making the rounds at every concession stand and hanging out with the baseball siblings between games. With her outgoing personality, competitive spirit, and love for sports, Callie is a valued teammate and friend who continues to make great memories both on and off the field.
