Aubrey

Aubrey is a talented young athlete competing in 10U softball and currently playing for the South Hills Elite 9U travel team. A true utility player, Aubrey has the ability to contribute anywhere on the field and is always ready to step into whatever role her team needs. Known for her quick bat and exceptional contact hitting, she consistently puts the ball in play and creates opportunities for her team. While she primarily plays first base for South Hills Elite, she is also the number two pitcher for her GBWAA team, showcasing her versatility and athleticism.

Beyond her skills on the field, Aubrey is known for her amazing personality and ability to make friends wherever she goes. Her positive attitude and friendly nature naturally draw people toward her, making her a favorite teammate and friend. When she's not playing softball, Aubrey enjoys visiting Kennywood, jumping on the trampoline, playing basketball, and spending time with friends. Her enthusiasm, work ethic, and love for the game make her an exciting player to watch and a valued member of every team she joins.
