Lucca

Ace pitcher, competitor, teammate, and Devil Dogs 10U player.

Lucca is a talented young athlete competing in 10U softball and currently playing for the Devil Dogs 10U travel team. As the team's ace pitcher, she brings confidence, determination, and poise every time she takes the circle. A returning pitcher from last year's 10U All-Star team, Lucca has already proven herself as a dependable competitor who thrives in big moments. Her strong work ethic, passion for the game, and commitment to improving continue to make her a key leader and valuable teammate.

When she's not on the softball field, Lucca enjoys expressing her creativity through crafting, participating in cheer, and listening to the musical Hamilton. She also loves spending time with friends and is especially known for hosting fun pool parties. Her outgoing personality, positive attitude, and competitive spirit make her someone teammates and friends enjoy being around. With her talent, confidence, and love for the game, Lucca has an exciting future ahead both on and off the softball field.
