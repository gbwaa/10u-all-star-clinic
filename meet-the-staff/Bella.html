10U All-Star Staff

Bella

Bella is a returning 10U All-Star and a pitcher known for her positive attitude, strong work ethic, and love for the game. She brings energy and enthusiasm to every practice and game and is the kind of teammate who makes everyone feel welcome. Bella talks to everyone, can strike up a conversation with anyone, and has a natural ability to make people feel included and valued. Her upbeat personality, kind heart, and encouraging nature make her a favorite among teammates and coaches alike. Bella’s dedication to improving her skills while supporting those around her continues to help her grow as both a player and leader on the field.

When she’s not playing softball, Bella stays active with travel basketball, choir, and band, and is involved with PBIS at Lutz Elementary. She also loves dancing, singing, and spending time with her family and friends. One of Bella’s favorite softball memories is having the opportunity to pitch in the 8U Fastpitch World Series, an experience that fueled her passion for the game. Looking ahead, Bella dreams of continuing her softball career at the college level, following in the footsteps of her older sister, who plays collegiate softball at Pitt–Johnstown. With her athletic ability, outgoing personality, and dedication both on and off the field, Bella has a bright future ahead.
