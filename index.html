Build confidence, sharpen skills, and have fun.

Join the GBWAA 10U All-Stars for a fastpitch softball clinic designed for young athletes who want to learn the game the right way. Players will rotate through upbeat stations focused on defense, grounders, hitting, pitching fundamentals, teamwork, and game-ready confidence.

What players will work on

  • Ready position, fielding posture, and active feet.
  • Ground-ball reads, glove presentation, and strong throws.
  • Fastpitch hitting approach, balance, contact, and follow-through.
  • Pitching basics including grip, arm circle, stride, and finish.
  • Encouragement from current 10U All-Star players.

Fastpitch fundamentals taught with energy and encouragement

This clinic is built around the skills young softball players need most: a strong foundation, lots of touches, positive coaching, and the confidence to try hard things. The GBWAA 10U All-Stars will help demonstrate drills and cheer on the next wave of 6U and 8U players.

🥎

Defense & Fielding

Players will practice athletic ready position, moving through the ball, glove angles, two-hand security, and quick transitions into throwing. The goal is to make defense feel simple, active, and fun.

🧤

Grounders & Throws

Ground-ball stations will focus on reading hops, staying low, using soft hands, and making accurate throws to a target. Players will learn how small details create cleaner plays in games.

🏏

Hitting

Hitters will work on stance, load, timing, balanced swings, contact through the ball, and finishing strong. Drills will keep players moving while helping them understand how to attack good pitches.

💪

Pitching Basics

Fastpitch pitching instruction will introduce grip, arm circle, stride direction, release point, and follow-through in an age-appropriate way. Beginners are welcome, and the focus will be on safe mechanics and confidence.

⭐

Leadership

Clinic participants will learn from 10U All-Star players who remember what it felt like to be new to the game. The day is about building skills while showing younger players what teamwork looks like.

📣

All-Star Fundraiser

Every sign-up supports the GBWAA 10U Fastpitch All-Star team. Thank you for helping these players represent their community while giving back to younger softball families.
