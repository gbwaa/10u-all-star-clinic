Amelia

Amelia is a talented young athlete currently playing for South Hills Elite. Known for her power at the plate and strong arm in the field, Amelia is a player who can make an impact every time she steps onto the diamond. She also serves as the number one catcher for her GBWAA team, where her toughness, leadership, and knowledge of the game help guide her teammates. Her dedication, determination, and love for softball continue to help her grow into a standout young player.

Off the field, Amelia is known for her great personality and for always being unapologetically herself. She enjoys playing basketball, swimming, and spending time running around the neighborhood with friends. During the summer, you can often find her gathered around a backyard campfire with her buddies, making memories and enjoying the outdoors. Her confidence, positive attitude, and competitive spirit make her someone teammates and friends enjoy being around. With her passion for sports and natural leadership qualities, Amelia has a bright future ahead both on and off the field.
