10U All-Star Staff

Aniya

Aniya is a talented young athlete and Devil Dogs 10U player known for being a big power hitter and a strong corner infielder who mostly plays third base and can also step in at first. After a knee injury and surgery took her away from softball for the 2025 spring and summer season, Aniya worked hard to get her skills back on track and kept pushing herself to return stronger. She joined a travel organization, continued building her confidence, and hit her first grand slam home run this year, showing the determination and toughness she brings to the field. Whether she's driving the ball, making plays at the corners, or supporting her teammates, Aniya brings focus, energy, and a love for the game every time she steps on the diamond.

Off the field, Aniya loves spending time with her friends, reading a good book, riding her bike, and listening to music. She also plays travel basketball and is part of the PBIS team at Lutz Elementary, helping bring students the news and happenings around the building. Aniya is the kind of teammate and friend who makes an effort to help everyone feel comfortable, pick people up, and keep everyone laughing with a fantastic sense of humor. With her outgoing personality, competitive spirit, and dedication to growing as an athlete, Aniya continues to make a positive impact both on and off the field.
