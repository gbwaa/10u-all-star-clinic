Izzy

Izzy is a talented young athlete competing in 10U softball and currently playing for the South Hills Elite 9U travel team. As a pitcher, she brings determination, toughness, and a fierce competitive spirit every time she steps onto the field. Izzy is known for her work ethic, confidence, and ability to rise to the occasion in big moments. Her passion for the game and drive to improve continue to make her a valuable teammate and exciting player to watch.

Off the field, Izzy enjoys spending time relaxing on her tablet, playing with and caring for cats, and practicing tumbling. She also loves making memories and having fun with her two sisters during her free time. With her strong personality, competitive mindset, and love for sports, Izzy has a bright future ahead both on and off the softball field.
