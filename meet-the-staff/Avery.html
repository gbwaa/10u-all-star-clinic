Avery

Avery is a talented young athlete and a key member of the Devil Dogs 10U travel team. Known for her strong swing, impressive power at the plate, and dependable glove in the field, Avery is a player who can make an impact in every part of the game. Her dedication to improving her skills and her love for softball continue to help her develop into a valuable teammate and competitor. Whether she's making a big defensive play or driving the ball into the outfield, Avery brings confidence and energy every time she steps onto the field.

Off the diamond, Avery is known for her fun-loving attitude and positive personality, making her a joy to be around. When she's not playing softball, she enjoys cheerleading and gymnastics, where she showcases her athleticism and enthusiasm. She also loves shopping, swimming, and spending time with her dogs at home. With her bright smile, strong work ethic, and passion for sports, Avery has an exciting future ahead both on and off the field.
