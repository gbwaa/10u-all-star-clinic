Makenzie

Catcher, power hitter, teammate, and South Hills Elite 9U player.

Makenzie is a talented young athlete competing in 10U softball and currently playing for the South Hills Elite 9U travel team. As a catcher, she brings confidence, toughness, and leadership behind the plate. Known for her strong arm and powerful bat, Makenzie is a difference-maker on both defense and offense. Her determination, work ethic, and love for the game continue to help her develop into a key contributor and dependable teammate.

Beyond her athletic abilities, Makenzie is known for her positive personality and ability to lift up those around her. Whether celebrating a big play or encouraging a teammate after a tough inning, she has a natural way of bringing energy and confidence to her team. Off the softball field, Makenzie enjoys playing basketball and spending time with family and friends. Her upbeat attitude, competitive spirit, and dedication to her teammates make her a player who is respected both on and off the field, with a bright future ahead in sports.
